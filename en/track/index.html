Husky

19
“Партизан”2025album
About the album

The album “Партизан” became one of the most important projects of our whole career. Work on it began in 2021 and wrapped up in 2025. Most of it was done in person, taking each track slowly and carefully to its final form.

  1. Живая вода
    About the work on this track

    “Живая вода” felt like a potential hit, so we wanted to make it as danceable as possible and bring out the drums and the horns. The main sonic accent here is the brass section. The mix came together very quickly and the artist liked it almost immediately.

  2. Ветром Снегом Зноем
    About the work on this track

    “Ветром Снегом Зноем” is the album's closing track. It took a long time because there were so many vocal parts. We wanted it to feel like the intro to an old Soviet TV series about the police. While mixing, we put such series up on a big screen for inspiration and aimed for the strongest possible sense of nostalgia.

    The biggest challenge was mixing the beatbox: a dry beatbox is not easy to turn into a convincing drum sound, and at the same time the track had to sit with the rest of the album.

  3. Музыка рай
    About the work on this track

    Work on “Музыка рай” went unusually: several tracks were originally written over this beat, and we mixed one of them almost completely. Then the lyrics were rewritten entirely and the meaning changed. The result was the version that came out on streaming.

    The hardest part was creating a viscous atmosphere in the style of dungeon synth while keeping the track listenable. We worked a lot on the vocal, experimented with pitch, with the space around the falsettos and backing vocals, and on the master we aimed for a pleasant tube-style clipping.

  4. Я боюсь
    About the work on this track

    “Я боюсь” turned out to be one of the simplest tracks in terms of mixing, because the demo was already well put together. Anton did the main mix, and Pavel separately worked on the tricks and effects that give the song its particular atmosphere.

    We wanted to create effects that had not existed before. More than fifty variants were made, and three or four remained in the final mix. We like the simplicity of this mix: the voice sounds plain, but good. In lyric-centered rap the main task is to make sure the listener hears every word.

  5. Танцы на снегу
    About the work on this track

    A key is the decision without which the mix does not work, and after which the track comes together and starts sounding the way it should. One key, once found, can close dozens of small revisions.

    The mix of “Танцы на снегу” came together only after we put iZotope Exciter on the master, running at almost 100%. Normally we never use saturators on the master bus that aggressively, but without the explosion typical of baile funk this mix did not work. Neither a clipper nor any other tool gave the needed effect at the start of the chorus.

    Baile funk largely took shape in the poorer neighborhoods of Brazil, where producers deliberately oversaturate their tracks. That is where the signature sound comes from, and it is impossible to get it through careful processing alone.

  6. Быть орком
    About the work on this track

    This was one of the hardest tracks on the album to mix: while it was being made, the beat changed genre and the vocal takes were re-recorded. In total I mixed four different versions of “Быть орком”.

    Dmitry Husky is an unusual artist: when other people rework material many times, the final version often ends up strained. Dmitry, on the contrary, is exactly the kind of artist who brings an idea to completion through several iterations. For a long time we could not find the right character for the bass, but careful saturation and Melda PolySaturator helped find the harmonics it needed.

  7. Я объявляю вам войну
    About the work on this track

    We started mixing this track together with Dmitry in Saint Petersburg at the Mix Room studio. The idea was to make a bright trap track. We drew on the sound we had already found for “Колобок” and aimed for the same bright, overcooked character.

    The track uses the same sample as “Танцы на снегу”. That ties the album's sound together and makes it more interesting to listen through.

  8. Контроль
    About the work on this track

    Mixing “Контроль” began at Dmitry's studio in Moscow, where I did a rough mix of the project in FL Studio. It did not come out perfectly right away, because I had hardly ever worked in that program before. This is one of the last tracks we mixed for the album.

    Once the idea of the mix had been assembled in Moscow, I finished it in my usual Studio One in Saint Petersburg. After several rounds of revisions the track was done. The goal was to make a live banger that people would dance to.

  9. Колобок
    About the work on this track

    “Колобок” was the first track I mixed after the Mix With The Masters seminar with Andrew Scheps in 2023. I made the first version on headphones in a wooden house in the Leningrad region, where Dmitry was holding his camp. Then we finished the track at the Mixroom studio.

    The shouts of “Они хотят меня сожрать” sound especially interesting. Andrew Scheps often uses SansAmp on various instruments, and I decided to try that technique. Although he did not put SansAmp on vocals at the seminar, I used it exactly there and got an expressive overdrive.

    Another difficulty was a very top-heavy bass that took up almost the entire frequency range. The main task was to tame it while keeping the power of the track.

  10. Лимонка
    About the work on this track

    While working on “Лимонка”, Dmitry gave no references, so I chose them myself. I wanted a dry sound in the style of Pharrell Williams and N.E.R.D., with clean transients and expressive percussion. I think it worked.

    The main difficulty was making a large number of ad-libs, shouts, backing vocals, and different sections of the song get along. There were many ways to arrange them, and the tenth version went to release.

  11. Партизан
    About the work on this track

    This track was very hard to mix: the sample takes up almost the entire frequency range, a massive bass sits right next to it, and the guttural vocal potentially conflicts with both the bass and the sample. We only managed to resolve that conflict in the third year of work.

    There were many project versions: we passed them back and forth, exported individual stems, EQ'd them, and re-recorded the vocal. The chorus sounded right straight away, powerful and loud. The main difficulty was in the verses. A fun fact: Anton recorded the whistle for the last chorus.

  12. Душа
    About the work on this track

    We mixed the first version of “Душа” in 2021. The job was closed then, and Dmitry was happy with everything. We came back to the track in 2025: in the meantime we had grown a lot as engineers, and the old mix no longer matched the rest of the album.

    Mixing “Душа” is a world of its own: it has an enormous number of sections and instruments, and the amount of work is unlike any other track. In total we spent several weeks on it. I especially like how we managed to place the trombones close to the listener in the space while making them sound slightly eerie. The priest's singing at the end of the track also stuck in my memory.

  13. Ангел учится летать
    About the work on this track

    I split “Ангел учится летать” into two parts. The opening is classic baile funk: reggaeton-flavored and slightly viscous. Here the attention goes first of all to the drums and transients. A soft clipper sits on the track's main bus and toasts the sound nicely.

    In the second part the drums disappear. As a child I saw a TV ad for Dior Fahrenheit set to very beautiful music with a pad that scattered across the space. Only years later did I find that piece: “As Falls Wichita, So Falls Wichita Falls” by Pat Metheny and Lyle Mays. Around the fourteenth minute there is an astonishing moment that feels like heaven. The ending of “Ангел учится летать” resembles it a little, and I tried to bring the sound closer to that association, which had left a deep mark on me.

  14. Я хочу быть псом
    About the work on this track

    By the time we got to this track we had already found the toasted, powerful sound of “Я объявляю вам войну” and “Колобок”. Here it was important to bring out the very loud backing vocals with the dog howl, “у-у”. In the demo they were pushed forward, and that shock balance had to be preserved in the mix.

    We paid a lot of attention to the overdriven percussion. Dmitry gave no references, but for some of the engineering decisions I drew on “Power Is Power” and other tracks from the Black Panther album.

  15. Интро
    About the work on this track

    The intro was made quickly and in one breath, right at the studio. Dmitry Husky himself and Yama Nora handled the production, so what we mostly had to do was the stem mastering.

MORGENSHTERN*

10
«ALISHER»2025album
About the album

The album came to us through Andrey Katikov. We had worked with Alisher for a long time by then, starting with the track “Грустно” with Magic Man and the album LAST ONE. Work on ALISHER began with “ДОМ (Лондон, Прага, Ницца)”. The deadline was tight, so one day we woke up at seven in the morning and worked until one at night. The mix was approved, and we got the chance to mix the whole album.

Alisher pays close attention to mixing: we discussed revisions on Zoom calls and went through options together with the artist and the producer. The music was recorded live, so it was important to handle the material carefully on the technical side. In total, mixing the album took about a month.

Interestingly, the album was recorded at the same time in a single room at a studio in Georgia. The instruments naturally bled into one another, which gave the music a cohesive, live character. When an album is recorded in one room, with the same instruments and in a single style, it helps to carry successful drum processing and sends from track to track, and the album sounds more of a piece.

We used techniques from Andrew Scheps that I learned at a Mix With The Masters seminar: parallel processing and parallel drum compression. For the vocal space we used a lot of mono chamber reverbs, including Hitsville Chambers from UAD, to give the album an old-school character. The melodic instruments were panned wide, so their parts read clearly. I especially like how that sounds on “КРАСНЫЙ ФЛАГ”.

DEVO4KA · with glaive2023
About the work on this track

What made the track interesting is that it is a feature with an American artist. The main difficulty was the kick: we wanted it as punchy as possible, with a dirt that works for the track. I even took a lesson from electronic musician Sergey Vospi so he could help synthesize the kick that would work best in this track.

For detailed sound design I brought in Ain Rei. Originally there were more than a hundred effects, but after revisions about fifteen remained, including the vocal chops. A whole team worked on the song.

The mix sounds dirty, and I still like the result, because that dirt is deliberate. glaive's vocal was recorded fairly overdriven to begin with, but in the mix we managed to seat it organically.
