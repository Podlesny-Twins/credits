“Встретимся завтра”

“Встретимся завтра” (Vstretimsya Zavtra, 2025) — T-Fest, катя милтей (katya miltey). Mixing: Podlesny Twins (Pavel and Anton Podlesny).

The project came directly from T-Fest. Working with him involves a lot of joint searching; together we bring the song to its precise character.

The main task was to blend the voices of T-Fest and катя милтей (Katya Miltey) harmoniously while preserving the individuality of each timbre.
