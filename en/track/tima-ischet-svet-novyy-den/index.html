“новый день”

“новый день” (Novyy Den, 2025) — тима ищет свет (tima ishchet svet). Mixing and mastering: Podlesny Twins (Pavel and Anton Podlesny).

We have worked with Timofey since 2020, starting with the album “Зеркало”. He pays close attention to mixing; we fine-tune many of the details together on calls.

On the acoustic guitars we tried a bitcrusher, a technique we brought back from Mix With The Masters.
