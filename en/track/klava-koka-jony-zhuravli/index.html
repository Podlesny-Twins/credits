“Журавли”

“Журавли” (Zhuravli, 2026) — Klava Koka, JONY. Mastering: Podlesny Twins (Pavel and Anton Podlesny).

Artyom Kassa produced “Журавли” (https://kaccamusic.com/). He also mixed Klava Koka's part, and Alex Davia mixed JONY's part. We did the stem mixing.

The main work was on the chorus. It was important to make sure the artists' timbres, layered on top of each other, did not create an excess of energy in the upper mids. For precise, detailed control of the resonances we used SpecCraft, Soothe 3, and Mastering The Mix Resonance. We also checked that frequency range on the studio's Auratone Cube, which exposes these problems particularly well.
